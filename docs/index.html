1Executive summary

  1. On every real SMS text the filter holds (275, from a public research dataset, answers committed before the run) the agents scored 251/275, and the system released none of 157 real scams. On new held-out sets written before running, v7 scored 8/8 and v8 (6 of 10 in Arabic) 10/10, with 0 and 0 harmful decisions. On 5,971 real SMS the message checks flag 79.2% of real smishing and 0.1% of legitimate messages.
  2. Without governance, 10 genuine health and government messages were silently lost and 2 health-data transfers left the country. With HISN: 0 and 0; held messages reach a named human, then a second reviewer within 15 minutes.
  3. On ITU's reference model, an ungoverned LLM scored 16/33 with 41 unsupported legal citations, below fixed rules (23/33). HISN scored 26/33 (held-out v2 4/4, v3 1/2) with 1 harmful decision(s), against 10 for the LLM alone.
  4. Testing candidate rules: sender authentication with a verified-sender lane raised the MIZAN balance from 76.7 to 94.2; a blunt fraud duty lowered it (76.2), over-blocking legitimate businesses.
  5. The regulatory wind tunnel tests draft rules before issuance: the same emergency-offshore provision is rejected as a regulation (conflicts with Federal Law No. 2 of 2019, Art. 13) and compatible as a Health Authority resolution, the route Article 13 itself provides.
  6. 7 domain-level policy gaps were identified, one discovered by the agents; four are shared or only partly addressed in India (UAE-India decision agreement 56%).

9Method and limitations

  • Scenario messages are synthetic; four evaluations use real public data. In the real SMS set, sender IDs and authentication are simulated (no public data has them). Arabic legal texts are OCR and Arabic checks await native review.
  • Accuracies are shown with 95% Wilson confidence intervals; with samples this small the intervals are wide.
  • Pre-registered expectations committed to version control before each held-out run; a smaller model overfitted the development set, the reference model did not.
  • A similarity threshold copied from the ITU reference agent did not transfer to this embedding model and is not claimed as a safeguard.
  • Comparator texts and the wind tunnel's conflict table require legal review; India institutional agents are future work.
  • This is a sandbox prototype for pre-issuance testing, not a production system.
