Governing the telecom AI that decides which health and government messages reach citizens

Assurance report on a working prototype, with pre-issuance testing of draft rules against verified UAE law.

ITU AI for Good Lab Hackathon, UAE 2026Team SankalpSynthetic dataAudit chains verified
Independent hackathon prototype. This is not an official government publication and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any UAE or Indian government entity. All senders and data are synthetic.

1Executive summary

  1. Without governance, 10 genuine health and government messages were silently lost and 2 health-data transfers left the country. With HISN: 0 and 0; held messages reach a named human, then a second reviewer within 15 minutes.
  2. On ITU's reference model, an ungoverned LLM scored 16/33 with 41 unsupported legal citations, below fixed rules (23/33). HISN scored 26/33 (held-out v2 4/4, v3 1/2) with zero harmful decisions.
  3. Testing candidate rules: sender authentication with a verified-sender lane raised the MIZAN balance from 76.7 to 94.2; a blunt fraud duty lowered it (76.2), over-blocking legitimate businesses.
  4. The regulatory wind tunnel tests draft rules before issuance: the same emergency-offshore provision is rejected as a regulation (conflicts with Federal Law No. 2 of 2019, Art. 13) and compatible as a Health Authority resolution, the route Article 13 itself provides.
  5. 7 domain-level policy gaps were identified, one discovered by the agents; four are shared or only partly addressed in India (UAE-India decision agreement 56%).

5Test-driven regulation: the regulatory wind tunnel

A draft provision in plain language is mapped onto testable mechanisms (proposed by a model, validated by code, confirmed by a human), checked against verified law including the hierarchy of norms, regression-tested against nine public-interest invariants, and measured. Each result is sealed in the audit chain. Drafts are illustrative and demonstrate the method; HISN makes no national recommendations, and AI only validates impact while humans confirm and decide (Readiness Report 2.0, FAQ 7).

9Method and limitations

  • Synthetic data and scoring weights: results show direction, not real-world magnitudes.
  • Accuracies are shown with 95% Wilson confidence intervals; with samples this small the intervals are wide.
  • Pre-registered expectations committed to version control before each held-out run; a smaller model overfitted the development set, the reference model did not.
  • A similarity threshold copied from the ITU reference agent did not transfer to this embedding model and is not claimed as a safeguard.
  • Comparator texts and the wind tunnel's conflict table require legal review; India institutional agents are future work.
  • This is a sandbox prototype for pre-issuance testing, not a production system.
