# Network stressor evidence (Turkcell ITU AI/ML in 5G Challenge 2021 data, used as proxy)

Profile: kb/rulepacks/network_profile.yaml (generated by tools/build_rlf_profile.py; raw data not redistributed).

| condition | failures per link-day | relative risk |
|---|---|---|
| baseline | 0.10% | - |
| hot days (>= 30.4 C, top 10%) | 0.057% | 0.54 |
| rainy days (>= 5 mm) | 0.24% | 4.49 |

985,308 link-days, 985 failures, 1,674 radio sites joined to their nearest of 20 weather stations; median outage 5.9 min.
Generated injects (2,000 links, seed 42): storm_rainfade 14 failures in 3 days (~4.7/day); heatwave_links 13 in 7 days (~1.9/day).

Finding: heat did not increase backhaul link failures in this data; rain increased them about 4.5x. Hot days are largely
dry, so heat and the absence of rain are confounded. Act 1's network stressor is therefore an operator energy-saving
decision, not a weather-driven failure; the storm_rainfade inject uses the measured rain-day rate.
Proxy caveat: Turkish operator microwave backhaul, not UAE data.
Citation: Aktas S., Alemdar H., Ergut S. (2022) Computers & Electrical Engineering 99:107742.
