_Generated from `datasets/hisn-synthetic-sms/hisn_synthetic_sms.csv` by `tools/build_datasheet.py`. Structure follows Gebru et al., Datasheets for Datasets; metrics follow the ITU AI Readiness Report 2.0 data dimension._

## Motivation
Built to evaluate governance of telecom SMS filtering for essential health and government messages, and published because ITU's sandbox datasets contain no SMS scam data. Created by Team Sankalp for the ITU AI for Good Lab Hackathon (UAE).

## Collection and labelling
- Fully synthetic: written for the scenario, no real messages, senders or people. Labels assigned by construction.
- Fictional senders only; no real phishing content is generated.
- URL safety check: 40 URL(s) found; non-reserved domains: none.
